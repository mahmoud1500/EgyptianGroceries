## Licensing and attribution gate

Do not add external photos to the repository until the exact source file page, commercial-use license, attribution text, and any share-alike requirements have been checked. A search result or an image preview alone is not sufficient proof of permission. Record source URL, creator, license/version, required attribution, and local filename for every newly sourced image.

Existing vendor-supplied photos remain separate from marketplace/dish photos and retain their vendor-provided `photo_url` precedence.
